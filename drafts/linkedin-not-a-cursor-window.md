## Optional LinkedIn Article

**Title:** Grok Bot is not a Cursor window — and a second Bot is not a fence

**Visibility:** Public. Leave any “canonical / original URL” field blank.

If someone on your team says they “turned on Grok Bot in Cursor,” they are describing the wrong product.

Grok Bot is a separate app: named teammates plus one cloud computer that keeps running after you close the laptop. There is no extra Grok Bot account. You sign in with the Cursor account. Weekly usage is metered there. Plugins can inherit from that account. It is not a sidebar inside the IDE.

This is not an official xAI or Cursor post. Packages stay on https://x.ai/bot

### Brief finance from the originals

There is no consumer free tier and no standalone Grok Bot SKU. Access is bundled with a paid Cursor plan or a linkable personal SuperGrok / X plan. Linking SuperGrok is a usage grant on the same Cursor account. You cannot unlink it yourself.

On a single fetch day (4 September 2026), official pages still disagreed on Cursor Pro and base SuperGrok. Cursor Help and the 26 August news post included them. The xAI FAQ and Get started that day did not. Do not pick a winner from a blog. Open the three originals.

How the Cursor account relates to the Bot app — login, usage, inherited plugins, not an IDE panel — is written here:

https://grokbot.run/learn/cursor/

### Brief security from the computer page

The roster looks like a team. The computer is not.

Every Bot on the account uses the same persistent machine. Cookies, files, and logins are shared. Life Bot and work Bot share a browser. Official wording and a Cursor forum pin agree: isolation is per user, not per Bot. Screens are a work surface, not a security boundary.

If you would not put a credential on a shared laptop, do not put it on this computer. Do not treat a second Bot as a fence.

The longer write-up, with the forum pin and the official wording side by side:

https://grokbot.run/learn/computer/

### What to tell the team on day one

1. This is not grok.com and not a Cursor IDE panel.
2. Sign in with the org Cursor account. Do not open a personal account “just to try it.”
3. One Bot, one checkable job, stop at drafts. Do not send, delete, or pay from a copied brief.
4. Treat the computer as shared. Recover before Reset.

That is enough for a rollout note. Everything else is a specialist the team has not earned yet.
